The problem

Your to-do list is lying to you.

It says everything matters equally. It never says what ships today. It hands you forty things and lets you pick — which is exactly why you pick nothing.

Decision paralysis

Twenty minutes every morning deciding what to do. By the time you pick, the good hours are gone — spent on choosing, not doing.

Too big to start

“Launch the new homepage” is not a task — it is two weeks hiding in one line. No wonder the phone wins.

Invisible progress

You shipped three things this week. No record, no evidence — so your brain files it under “I did nothing.”

How it works

Three moves a day. That is the whole method.

01

Dump your mind

Type “call investor tomorrow 3pm urgent” and stop thinking about it. The parser pulls out the date, the priority, the energy, the project, the tags. No forms. No dropdowns.

@Engineering #urgent tomorrow 3pm
02

Get exactly one answer

Each morning you tell it how you feel — Admin, Medium, Deep. It returns a single task you are actually able to do right now, with a Start focus button sitting next to it. Not forty things. One.

Admin Medium Deep
03

Ship it, then see it

Tasks, streaks, focus sessions, energy logs and journal entries all roll into a weekly review. Friday stops being “what did I even do?” and starts being a receipt.

Who it is for

Built for builders, not bureaucrats.
