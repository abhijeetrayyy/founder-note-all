Product blueprint · v1

A founder does not have a task problem.
They have an open-loop problem.

Every existing tool measures how much you completed. That is the wrong number — it goes up when you add more, so it can never make you feel better. FounderOS measures the pressure in your head and is designed to bring it down. Everything below follows from that one decision.

Wrong unit

The unit is a loop, not a task

A worry, a decision you keep deferring, a person you owe a reply, a number you have not looked at. Tasks are only the loops that already got named.

Wrong direction

The list must be able to shrink

Loops decay. At seven days they go amber, at fourteen the app stops you and forces one of four answers. Nothing gets to sit there quietly generating guilt.

Wrong assumption

A day has capacity, not slots

Three deep units, four medium, eight admin. You cannot plan past it — the planner physically refuses. Burnout is an overcommit bug, so we fix it at input.

The loop

Six stages. Every screen in the app belongs to exactly one.

If a feature does not sit on this ring, it does not ship. That is how the app stays small while the problem stays big.
